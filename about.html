About Our Team

A student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team from Galați, Romania, building robots and growing a stronger STEM community.

Our Mission

Seramitae #23486 began four years ago at the “Costache Negri” National College, where two students and their physics teacher turned a shared passion for technology into a robotics club. Today, we compete in FIRST Tech Challenge and develop skills across mechanical design, programming, CAD, 3D printing, drive team, and outreach.

We build more than robots. Through projects such as Caravana Seramitae and STEM Beyond Borders, we create hands-on STEM experiences, mentor younger students, and collaborate with FIRST communities in Romania and abroad. Guided by our mentors, we learn to test ideas, improve through mistakes, and support one another as a team.
