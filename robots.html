Cipi

Into The Deep


This is the final version of our robot for the 2024-2025 FIRST Tech Challenge season, Into The Deep. With this robot, we competed in the National Championship and qualified for an international event, the Chicago Robotics Invitational. The robot features both vertical and horizontal extensions, runs a 5-specimen autonomous routine, scores 13 specimens during TeleOp for a total of 18 specimens, and is capable of a Level 2 Ascent.

Weight: 15kg
